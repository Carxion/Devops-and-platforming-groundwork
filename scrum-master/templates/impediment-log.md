## Status meanings

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Open | Known, not yet being worked on |
| In progress | The team or scrum master is working on it |
| Escalated | Needs someone outside the team; the owner is chasing them |
| Resolved | Removed. Note what changed, and whether the impact went away |
| Won't fix | Declined, with the reason. Tell the team who raised it |

## Escalation note

When escalating, send:

- **The impediment** in one sentence
- **The evidence:** time lost, items affected, cycle time, incidents
- **A proposal** or an experiment
- **The decision needed,** from whom, by when
