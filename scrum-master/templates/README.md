# Scrum master templates

[← Scrum master](../README.md)

Copy-ready templates for running Scrum (or Scrum with Kanban) on a DevOps, platform, or SRE team. Copy them into your organization's approved systems before filling them in, and adapt them freely. Don't fill them in here.

| Template | Use | Guide |
|---|---|---|
| [sprint-planning.md](sprint-planning.md) | A planning agenda and worksheet: capacity, interrupt reserve, Sprint Goal, and the forecast | [Running the events](../02-running-the-events.md#sprint-planning) |
| [retrospective-formats.md](retrospective-formats.md) | Six retrospective formats, and when each works best | [Running the events](../02-running-the-events.md#retrospective) |
| [working-agreement.md](working-agreement.md) | Prompts for the team's own agreement on how it works together | [Impediments and team health](../04-impediments-and-team-health.md#working-agreements) |
| [definition-of-done.md](definition-of-done.md) | A definition of done for infrastructure and platform work, including operational readiness | [Scrum for platform teams](../01-scrum-for-platform-teams.md) |
| [impediment-log.md](impediment-log.md) | A log of impediments with impact, owner, and escalation | [Impediments and team health](../04-impediments-and-team-health.md#removing-them) |
| [team-health-check.md](team-health-check.md) | A self-assessment the team runs every few months | [Impediments and team health](../04-impediments-and-team-health.md#team-health-checks) |

The [product owner templates](../../product-owner/templates/README.md) cover the backlog side: user stories, prioritization, the product goal, and the roadmap.
