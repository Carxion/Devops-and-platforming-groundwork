# Solutions architect templates

[← Solutions architect](../README.md)

Copy-ready templates for taking a solution from discovery to handover. Copy them into your organization's approved systems before filling them in, and adapt them freely. Don't fill them in here, and never put customer names or details in this repository.

| Template | Use | Guide |
|---|---|---|
| [discovery-guide.md](discovery-guide.md) | Questions for business, technical, and operations discovery sessions, and the written follow-up | [Discovery and requirements](../02-discovery-and-requirements.md) |
| [solution-design.md](solution-design.md) | The solution design document: drivers, requirements, options, the recommendation, cost, and risks | [Designing the solution](../03-designing-the-solution.md) |
| [proof-of-concept-plan.md](proof-of-concept-plan.md) | The question a POC answers, success criteria agreed in advance, the time box, and the result | [Designing the solution](../03-designing-the-solution.md#prove-whats-risky-proofs-of-concept) |
| [proposal.md](proposal.md) | A proposal structured to be decided on: executive summary, solution, scope, assumptions, cost, and next steps | [Preparing a proposal](../05-preparing-a-proposal.md) |
| [presentation-plan.md](presentation-plan.md) | One page to prepare a presentation: outcome, audience, story, likely questions, and logistics | [Presenting the solution](../06-presenting.md) |
| [delivery-handover.md](delivery-handover.md) | Everything the delivery team needs from the solutions architect, as a checklist and a record | [Working with stakeholders](../04-working-with-stakeholders.md#handing-over-to-delivery) |

- The architect's [decision matrix](../../architect/templates/decision-matrix.md), [quality attribute requirements](../../architect/templates/quality-attribute-requirements.md), and [architecture review checklist](../../architect/templates/architecture-review-checklist.md).
