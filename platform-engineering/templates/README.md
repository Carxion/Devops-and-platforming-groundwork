# Platform engineering templates

[← Platform engineering](../README.md)

Copy-ready templates for designing, running, and assessing a platform. Copy them into your organization's approved systems before filling them in, and adapt them freely. Don't fill them in here.

| Template | Use | Guide |
|---|---|---|
| [golden-path-definition.md](golden-path-definition.md) | Define one golden path with its users: who it's for, what it decides, escape hatches, support, and measures | [Golden paths](../03-golden-paths.md#designing-it-with-its-users) |
| [capability-map.md](capability-map.md) | What teams need, how each capability is provided today, and the gaps | [Building and running](../06-building-and-running-the-platform.md#map-the-capabilities) |
| [platform-service-definition.md](platform-service-definition.md) | What one platform capability offers, its interface and version, its SLOs, support, and deprecation policy | [Building and running](../06-building-and-running-the-platform.md#run-the-platform-as-a-product-in-production) |
| [platform-maturity-assessment.md](platform-maturity-assessment.md) | A self-assessment on the CNCF maturity model's five aspects, with evidence | [Adoption and measurement](../07-adoption-and-measurement.md#assess-maturity-aspect-by-aspect) |
| [developer-experience-survey.md](developer-experience-survey.md) | A short, repeatable survey of the platform's users | [Adoption and measurement](../07-adoption-and-measurement.md#what-to-measure) |

The [product owner templates](../../product-owner/templates/README.md) cover the product side: product goal canvas, user interview guide, roadmap, and release notes.
