# Manager templates

[← Manager](../README.md)

Copy-ready templates for running a DevOps, platform, or SRE team. Copy them into your organization's approved systems before filling them in, and adapt them freely. Don't fill them in here.

| Template | Use | Guide |
|---|---|---|
| [one-on-one.md](one-on-one.md) | A running, shared document per person, with questions for when the agenda is empty | [Running the team](../01-running-the-team.md#one-on-ones) |
| [career-ladder.md](career-ladder.md) | Levels and expectations for platform, DevOps, and SRE engineers, to adapt with HR | [Running the team](../01-running-the-team.md#expectations) |
| [interview-scorecard.md](interview-scorecard.md) | A scoring sheet and a question bank for structured interviews | [Hiring and onboarding](../02-hiring-and-onboarding.md#structured-interviews) |
| [onboarding-plan.md](onboarding-plan.md) | A new engineer's first 90 days, from before day one to joining on-call | [Hiring and onboarding](../02-hiring-and-onboarding.md#onboarding-new-engineers) |
| [quarterly-planning.md](quarterly-planning.md) | A capacity worksheet that accounts for run, improve, and build work | [Planning and metrics](../03-planning-and-metrics.md) |
| [on-call-policy.md](on-call-policy.md) | Rotation, expectations, escalation, compensation, and alert standards | [On-call and team health](../04-on-call-and-team-health.md) |
| [incident-review.md](incident-review.md) | A blameless review: impact, timeline, contributing factors, actions | [On-call and team health](../04-on-call-and-team-health.md#incident-reviews) |
| [team-charter.md](team-charter.md) | One page on what the team owns and how to work with it | [Stakeholders and communication](../05-stakeholders-and-communication.md) |

The [tech lead templates](../../tech-lead/templates/) are useful to managers too: the stakeholder map, weekly status, ADR, RFC, roadmap, risk register, and RACI.
