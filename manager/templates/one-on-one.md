## Questions for when the agenda is empty

Use one or two, not all of them.

- What's the most frustrating part of your work right now?
- What's getting in your way that I could remove?
- What would you change about how the team works?
- What have you learned recently? What do you want to learn next?
- Is there work you'd like more of, or less of?
- How's your on-call load? Anything paging you that shouldn't?
- What's something you did recently that you're proud of, and who should know about it?
- Where do you want to be in a year? What would help you get there?
- How am I doing? What's one thing I could do differently?
