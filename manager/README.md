# Manager

[← DevOps and Platforming Groundwork](../README.md)

For engineering managers and leads of DevOps, platform, and SRE teams: the people side of the work the rest of this repository covers. New to the team? Start with [your first 90 days](00-first-90-days.md), the manager's counterpart to the [tech lead checklist](../tech-lead/README.md). The other guides are about running the team week after week.

Platform and operations teams have problems product teams don't: interrupt-driven work, on-call, invisible success ("nothing broke"), and stakeholders who are other engineers. Each guide takes those into account.

| Guide | Covers |
|---|---|
| [Your first 90 days as a manager](00-first-90-days.md) | A W1/30/60/90 checklist for taking over a team: people, the work, on-call, stakeholders, team health, budget, and what to deliver by day 90 |
| [Running the team](01-running-the-team.md) | One-on-ones, expectations, feedback, growth, team rituals, and written culture |
| [Hiring and onboarding](02-hiring-and-onboarding.md) | Defining roles, structured interviews and work samples, and a first-90-days plan for new engineers |
| [Planning and metrics](03-planning-and-metrics.md) | Balancing planned work with interrupts and toil, roadmaps, technical debt, and measuring delivery with the DORA metrics |
| [On-call and team health](04-on-call-and-team-health.md) | Sustainable rotations, alert hygiene, incident reviews, and spotting burnout |
| [Stakeholders and communication](05-stakeholders-and-communication.md) | Status updates, decisions, escalations, and making the platform's value visible |
| [Performance and promotions](06-performance-and-promotions.md) | Goals, reviews without bias, supporting someone who's struggling, building a promotion case, recognition |
