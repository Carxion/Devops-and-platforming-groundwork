# Product owner templates

[← Product owner](../README.md)

Copy-ready templates for owning a platform or internal tool as a product. Copy them into your organization's approved systems before filling them in, and adapt them freely. Don't fill them in here.

| Template | Use | Guide |
|---|---|---|
| [product-goal.md](product-goal.md) | A one-page canvas: users, their problems, the product goal, how you'll measure it, and what's out of scope | [The platform as a product](../01-the-platform-as-a-product.md#the-product-goal) |
| [user-story.md](user-story.md) | A backlog item: the story, context, acceptance criteria, and notes for the team | [Backlog management](../02-backlog-management.md#user-stories) |
| [prioritization.md](prioritization.md) | WSJF and RICE worksheets, with scales | [Prioritization](../03-prioritization.md) |
| [roadmap.md](roadmap.md) | A now/next/later roadmap tied to the product goal | [Roadmaps and stakeholders](../04-roadmaps-and-stakeholders.md#now-next-later) |
| [user-interview-guide.md](user-interview-guide.md) | Questions and an approach for interviewing platform users | [The platform as a product](../01-the-platform-as-a-product.md#discovery-learning-what-users-need) |
| [release-notes.md](release-notes.md) | Release notes written for the platform's users | [Roadmaps and stakeholders](../04-roadmaps-and-stakeholders.md) |

The [scrum master templates](../../scrum-master/templates/README.md) cover the team's process: sprint planning, retrospectives, and the definition of done. The [tech lead templates](../../tech-lead/templates/) include a stakeholder map and an RFC.
