# Product owner

[← DevOps and Platforming Groundwork](../README.md)

For product owners and product managers of platforms and internal tools: deployment pipelines, developer platforms, shared infrastructure, observability, and the self-service that other engineering teams use. New to the role? Start with [your first 90 days](00-first-90-days.md).

A platform's customers are other engineers. They can often route around it, building their own pipeline or calling the cloud directly, so the platform has to earn its adoption like any other product. They're also colleagues who'll walk over and ask for things, which makes it easy to run the backlog as a queue of requests instead of a product with a goal. These guides are about the second job: deciding what the platform should become, and proving it got there.

## Working with the other roles

The product owner decides *what* the team works on and *why*. The [scrum master](../scrum-master/README.md) owns how the team works, the [engineering manager](../manager/README.md) the people, and the [tech lead](../tech-lead/README.md) the technical direction. The Scrum Guide's view of the role is the source here: one person, not a committee, accountable for maximizing the value of the product.
